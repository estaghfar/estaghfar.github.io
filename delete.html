ما الذي يُحذف

  • وثائقُ مزامنتك كلُّها تحت users/{معرّفك}/sync — أي مجاميعُ أذكارك اليومية وأوقاتُ آخر تسجيلٍ فيها.
  • سجلُّ حسابك في Firebase Authentication — بريدُك الإلكتروني ومعرّفُ حسابك.

ولا يبقى بعده شيءٌ عنك في السحابة. والحذفُ لا رجعةَ فيه.

ما الذي لا يُحذف بهذا — وكيف تمحوه

  • ما على جهازك (عدُّك وسجلُّك وإعداداتُك): يُمحى بإلغاء تثبيت التطبيق، أو: إعدادات النظام ← التطبيقات ← «مُذكِّر الاستغفار» ← التخزين ← مسح البيانات.
  • نسختُك الاحتياطية في حساب جوجل (إن كان نسخُ أندرويد الاحتياطيُّ مفعّلاً): تُدار من إعدادات جهازك ومن حسابك على جوجل — ولا نصل إليها.

ولا نحتفظ بأيّ نسخةٍ بعد الحذف، إلّا ما يُلزمنا به نظامٌ ساري المفعول — ولا شيءَ من ذلك قائمٌ اليوم.

Delete your account and data — “Istighfar Reminder”

App: Istighfar Reminder (مُذكِّر الاستغفار) · com.kaf.istighfar
Publisher: Ammar Alshaer · Contact: ammarfor@gmail.com
Last updated: 2026-11-05

Option 1 — inside the app (fastest)

Settings → ☁ Google sync → “🗑 Delete my account and data” → confirm. The button deletes your sync documents, then your Firebase account, clears the session data from your device, and tells you the result. It is immediate.

Option 2 — from this page, without installing the app

If you have already uninstalled the app, you do not need to install it again. Send an e-mail to ammarfor@gmail.com from the same address you signed in with (this is what proves the account is yours), with the subject Delete account — Istighfar Reminder. Nothing else is required: there is no subscription, no payment and no prior step — the app is free.

What is deleted

  • All your sync documents under users/{your-id}/sync — your daily dhikr totals and the time of the last entry of each day.
  • Your account record in Firebase Authentication — your e-mail address and account ID.

Nothing about you remains in the cloud afterwards. Deletion cannot be undone.

What this does not delete — and how to remove it

  • Data on your device (your count, log and settings): uninstall the app, or go to System settings → Apps → “Istighfar Reminder” → Storage → Clear data.
  • Your Android backup in your own Google account (if automatic backup is on): managed from your device settings and your Google account — we have no access to it.

Timing

In-app button: immediate. E-mail request: within 30 days at the most — in practice within days, and we confirm it by e-mail. We keep no copy after deletion, except where a law in force requires it — and none does today.
